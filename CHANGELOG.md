# Changelog

## 1.0.0
First public release.

- Album and playlist pages: whole-page tint from the cover colour, round Shuffle and + buttons with a wide Play pill,
  glass back button and top-right capsule, iOS 26 style info line, cover-coloured accents
- Word-by-word lyric highlighting, in sync with the song position
- Glass mini player capsule, with a clean pull-down animation for the full-screen player
- Lock screen: tap the album cover for an expanded player (full-size cover, colour backdrop, compact clock row, glass
  controls); the player sits lower so tall lock screen clocks are not cut off
- Home / New tab names, rounder covers and cards, profile circle colours
- App size slider with snap-to-default and reset buttons
- Safe mode (manual, and automatic after repeated early closes of Music)
- One Settings page for everything: Settings > Better Music
